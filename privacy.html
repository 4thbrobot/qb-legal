Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how 4145 Cindy Lane LLC ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with our QuickBooks Online integration Application.

1. Information We Access

Our Application accesses the following data from your QuickBooks Online account solely to perform the accounting functions you authorize: transaction records, expense entries, vendor information, account balances, and financial reports.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your QuickBooks data exclusively to: (a) record and categorize financial transactions; (b) generate accounting reports; and (c) maintain accurate books for your business. We do not use your data for any other purpose.

3. Data Storage

Your QuickBooks data is processed locally on your own hardware. We do not store your financial data on external servers or cloud services controlled by us beyond what QuickBooks itself retains.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise transfer your financial data to any third parties. Your data is used solely to provide the accounting automation services you have authorized.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your data, including encrypted credential storage and OAuth2 authentication. API credentials are stored locally and are not transmitted to third parties.

6. QuickBooks Data

Our access to your QuickBooks Online data is governed by Intuit's terms of service and privacy policy in addition to this policy. We access only the data scopes required for the functions you authorize.

7. Your Rights

You may revoke our Application's access to your QuickBooks account at any time through your Intuit account settings. Upon revocation, we will cease accessing your data.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
